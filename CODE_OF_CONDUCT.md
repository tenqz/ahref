# Code of conduct

Participation should be respectful, constructive and welcoming to people of all
backgrounds and experience levels. Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
Harassment, discriminatory language, threats and disclosure of private information
are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful contributions and restrict participation when
necessary. Report unacceptable behavior privately to smmartbiz@gmail.com. Reports
will be handled with care for the privacy of everyone involved.
